The Dubai Deals AE standard

Curated, not crowded.

Dubai Deals AE is a starter storefront concept for useful, giftable lifestyle products. The initial selection prioritises compact items that are easy to demonstrate in short videos and can potentially support a mid-range gross margin.

Product demand and supplier costs change. These are test ideas and pricing hypotheses—not verified sales forecasts. Validate demand with small batches, supplier samples and real customer feedback before investing in inventory.

Before going live
• Replace concept prices with supplier-verified costs.
• Add your real WhatsApp number and delivery/returns policy.
• Confirm product safety, warranty and UAE compliance.
• Set up a payment provider and order-management process.
• Use original or licensed product images for each SKU.
• Calculate margin after shipping, VAT, payment fees, ads and returns.
