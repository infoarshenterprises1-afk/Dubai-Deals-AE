DUBAI DEALS AE — PRODUCT TEST SHORTLIST (UAE / DUBAI)
Research snapshot: 11 October 2026

Why these categories:
- UAE e-commerce reached AED 42.2bn in 2025 and is forecast to continue growing (EZDubai / Dubai South, reported 28 Sep 2026).
- UAE shoppers increasingly use online channels for household and lifestyle purchases; UAE online FMCG grew 26.4% in Q2 2026 according to NielsenIQ reporting covered by Khaleej Times.
- Noon UAE trending/top-selling pages show demand across mobile accessories, small home appliances, beauty, fragrance, household and everyday essentials. Marketplace listings are a demand signal, not proof that a specific generic SKU will sell.

Test ideas and assumed economics (not verified supplier quotes):
1) Portable smoothie blender: target landed cost AED 55–65; test retail AED 99; gross margin approx 34–44%.
2) Wireless charging stand: target landed cost AED 45–58; test retail AED 89; gross margin approx 35–49%.
3) Motion-sensor night lamp: target landed cost AED 30–40; test retail AED 69; gross margin approx 42–57%.
4) Insulated travel tumbler: target landed cost AED 35–45; test retail AED 79; gross margin approx 43–56%.

Formula: gross margin = (selling price - landed product cost) / selling price.
Margins above exclude VAT, fulfilment, payment processing, ads, returns, warranty, discounts and overhead, so contribution margin will be lower.
